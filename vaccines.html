Book a vaccine

Seven short steps, about five minutes. Your screening answers and signed consent go straight to our pharmacist, so your visit takes minutes.

  • Most vaccines covered at no cost
  • Mon–Fri 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • No account needed

    Who is getting vaccinated?

    Booking for a child or family member? Enter their details here.

    How will this be covered?

    Most plans cover recommended vaccines at no cost. Have your card handy — or skip the numbers and bring it with you.

    Which vaccines do you need?

    Pick one or more. Options that don't fit the patient's age are greyed out. Not sure? Choose your best guess — the pharmacist will check your records.

    Quick health screening

    A "Yes" doesn't mean you can't be vaccinated — it just tells the pharmacist what to review with you.

    Pick a time

    Weekdays 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. This is your preferred time — we'll call or text to confirm it.

    Vaccine information & consent

    Please read the CDC Vaccine Information Statement (VIS) for each vaccine you selected. We'll also hand you a printed copy at your visit.

    Review & send

    Check everything is correct, then send your booking to the pharmacy.
